Delete your Kolab account

Last updated: 10 November 2026

You can permanently delete your Kolab account and the personal information linked to it. You do not need to contact us to do this, and it is free.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Kolab app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Your account is disabled immediately, your sessions are revoked and your profile and portfolio are removed from the app.

Delete by email

If you cannot sign in, email privacy@kolab.co.za from the email address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my Kolab account”. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account. We action verified requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we delete

  • Your name, email address and password hash.
  • Your bio, location, rates, measurements, photos and portfolio.
  • Your identity-verification files and verification notes.
  • Your follows, likes, saved searches and notification devices.
  • Your chat encryption keys and active sessions.
  • Your password-reset requests and in-app notifications.

What we keep, and why

  • Deals, invoices and payment records are kept for at least five years after the end of the relevant tax year, as required by South African tax law. After deletion they are stored against a “Deleted user” profile without your name, email, photo or contact details.
  • Reviews and messages that other users rely on may remain, but are no longer linked to a public, identifiable profile.
  • Security and crash logs are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted, unless needed to investigate fraud or a security incident.
  • Backups containing your data are overwritten within 30 days.

If you change your mind

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account. You are welcome to create a new account later with the same email address, but your previous profile, portfolio and history will not return.

Questions

Email privacy@kolab.co.za. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
