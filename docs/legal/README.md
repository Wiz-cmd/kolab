# Kolab legal & privacy pages

Static pages published with GitHub Pages from the `docs/` folder:

- `https://wiz-cmd.github.io/kolab/` – legal index
- `https://wiz-cmd.github.io/kolab/legal/privacy.html` – Privacy Policy (POPIA/GDPR)
- `https://wiz-cmd.github.io/kolab/legal/terms.html` – Terms of Service
- `https://wiz-cmd.github.io/kolab/legal/delete-account.html` – account deletion instructions

These URLs are hard-coded in the Android app's profile screen
(`ProfileScreen.kt`) and are the URLs to enter in Play Console.

## Before publishing to Google Play

1. Replace the trading-name-only references with the registered legal entity
   name, registration number and physical address.
2. Confirm that `privacy@kolab.co.za` and `support@kolab.co.za` are monitored.
3. Have a South African attorney review the Terms and the POPIA section.
4. Keep the “last updated” date accurate.
