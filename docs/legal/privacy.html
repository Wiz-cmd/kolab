Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Kolab (“Kolab”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the Kolab Android app and the Kolab API (together, the “Service”). We process personal information in line with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”) and, where applicable, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”).

Who is responsible?
Kolab is the responsible party (data controller) for the Service. Information Officer: Kolab Information Officer.
Email: privacy@kolab.co.za
Support: support@kolab.co.za

1. Information we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy we need it
Account information Name, email address, password hash, role (creator or brand), verification status To create and secure your account and to sign you in.
Profile and portfolio Bio, location, rates, measurements, profile and cover photos, portfolio images and captions, public encryption key To show your public profile and let brands and creators find each other.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. The Service is for adults. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

2. How we use information and our lawful basis

  • To provide the Service – performance of our contract with you.
  • To protect the Service and our users – our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and moderation.
  • To process payments and keep records – performance of a contract and compliance with tax and accounting law.
  • To verify your identity or portfolio – your explicit consent for sensitive information.
  • To send push notifications – your consent, which you can withdraw in Android settings.
  • To improve the Service – our legitimate interests, using aggregated or de-identified data where possible.

3. Sharing personal information

We share personal information only with:

  • Hosting and database providers that run the API and store the database.
  • Object-storage providers that store profile, portfolio and contract files.
  • PayFast and its banking partners, to process payments and payouts.
  • Push-notification providers, if you enable notifications.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and authorities, where required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.
  • Another user, when you choose to share information with them (for example by sending a message, applying to a deal or publishing a profile).
  • A successor entity, if Kolab is merged, acquired or sold, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your identity-verification documents publicly or with other users.

4. Cross-border transfers

Our hosting, database and storage providers may process information outside South Africa, including in the United States and the European Union. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we rely on POPIA section 72 (including the recipient being subject to laws or binding rules that provide substantially similar protection) and, for EU/UK data, appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. Retention

  • Account and profile data – until you delete your account.
  • Identity-verification files – deleted when verification is complete and the retention period for fraud prevention ends, or sooner on request where no legal duty requires retention.
  • Deals, invoices and payment records – kept for at least five years after the end of the tax year, as required by South African tax law, in anonymised form after account deletion.
  • Logs and crash data – normally no longer than 90 days, unless needed for a security investigation.
  • Backups – deleted or overwritten within 30 days.

6. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, encrypted storage for session credentials, hashed passwords, end-to-end encryption for chat messages and access controls on the API. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA (and the GDPR where it applies) you may:

  • ask for access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive or unlawfully obtained;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent for sensitive information or push notifications;
  • ask us to restrict processing or port your data where applicable;
  • complain to the Information Regulator.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@kolab.co.za. We may need to verify your identity first. We respond within 30 days.

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete Account. See Delete your account for details.
